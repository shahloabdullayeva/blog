And it's not even the same everywhere. There's one study I actually loved, Joan Miller asked Americans and Indians to explain why the people around them behave the way they do and the Americans explained everything through character, he did it because that's who he is, while the Indians explained the same behaviour through circumstances and the children of both countries answered almost the same so the difference grows with age which means it's learned (Miller, 1984).

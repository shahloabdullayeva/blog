I think trust is a very strange thing. You can know that someone loves you, know it for sure and still one small thing is enough to make you doubt it for a minute. Not because you actually believe it, you don't, you know it's not true even while it's happening but some part of you still needs to ask and hear it out loud. And when you ask it looks like you don't trust them. But I do!!! I'm just afraid to lose.

I used to think you can't have both that if you're afraid then you don't really trust. Now I think it's the opposite. You're only afraid of losing something when you finally have something worth losing. Nobody gets jealous over a person they don't care about. And of course it doesn't help when it's the emotional part of the month. Everything is louder I get angry first and think later, and things I would laugh at any other week suddenly feel like the end of the world. I know that's not an excuse and I'm not complaining it's just how it goes for me.

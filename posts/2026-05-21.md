# May 21, 2026

Someone introduced the word Linux to my life and that someone is no longer in it. I don't know if he knows what that one word did to me but I'm forever grateful. I installed Fedora and everything is messed up, why the hell did I even start it on my own? And that turned out to be the only way to actually learn something and life started to be interesting. Well now I can proudly say I know a little more than nothing now.

I've been thinking about death a lot lately and no I don't want to die. I have to say that first because every single time I bring this up people look at me like I need help. That's the whole problem right there.

We all die. It's the most guaranteed thing in existence more certain than anything else you can think of and somehow it's the one thing nobody wants to say out loud. You can talk about your salary your relationships your trauma your politics but the second you say the word death people freeze. They change the subject. They ask if you're okay. I'm okay. I'm just thinking. Is that not allowed? There's this book called The Denial of Death by Ernest Becker and he basically says that we're all aware we're going to die and everything we do is just us trying to cope with that. Our ambitions and our need to be remembered and our desperate attempts to leave something behind. Otto Rank called it Character Armor. We build this version of ourselves hoping some piece of it survives after we don't.

And honestly I think thinking about death makes life better not worse. When you accept that this whole thing ends you stop wasting time on stuff that doesn't matter. You look at the people around you differently. You stop acting like you have forever because you don't and you never did.

What's actually absurd is the avoidance. We treat death like saying the word out loud will summon it. Like it's not already here. Like it wasn't here before any of us showed up. Ignoring it doesn't make it disappear it just makes us less honest about everything. I should be able to sit somewhere and say we're all going to die without someone calling a therapist. Something is wrong only if you never think about it at all.

# August 27, 2026

Good news first because there is some. I am going back to eight hours for the same pay and I am writing it down so that a later version of me remembers that something once went her way. And then the part nobody warned me about, which is that the four hours came back to me as time to think and thinking is worse. Twelve hours was better. Twelve hours has no room in it and a day with no room in it cannot ask you any questions, you move and you answer and you move and by the time you are home there is nothing left of you to interview. Now there is a gap in the evening and the gap has opinions. The one nice thing I did with it is that I took the little script I made with Claude months ago, the one that did nothing but swap my profile picture when my shift started and we are evolving it and now it reads my entire shift and counts what I did and tells me where I was slow or short with somebody. So the first thing I built with my new free time was a thing that watches me. Make of that what you want.

Because here is what the free time actually brought up to the surface. I am becoming distant and it is not only me noticing it, people are staying away from me now too and I do not blame them for it. I feel bad being around my closest people. I love my parents and I love my friends and I mean that completely and I still do not want to be near them, I want them to exist but in the background, like weather I can hear from another room. And everything I touch becomes sick with sadness which is the only way I know how to say it. And when I do let somebody come closer they hurt me and I hurt them back, which looks ridiculous written down in one sentence like that, two people who love each other taking turns and I do not know what the hell is wrong with me or when it started or whether it is the job or the year or simply what I am now.

There is a very old story that is almost exactly this and Schopenhauer told it about porcupines. A group of them on a freezing day crowd together for the warmth and their quills go straight into each other so they move apart and then they are cold again so they move back in and get pricked again and they carry on shuffling in and out like that until they work out the middle distance where they can just about tolerate each other and just about not freeze and he says that politeness and manners are that middle distance and the whole of society is porcupines who have agreed on a spacing (Schopenhauer, 2000). I read it and felt understood for about a minute and then I saw the part that does not fit me. His porcupine still wants the warmth. It goes back in every single time and the only thing holding it back is the quills. That is not what I am describing. I am not keeping a careful distance because closeness hurts, I am out here because the fire stopped reading as warm and a porcupine that no longer feels the cold is not being polite, it is just a porcupine standing in a field on its own.

And why am I even writing this. I sat down tonight to make a nice post for once because I read my own archive back this week and it is a girl explaining month after month how bad life is and life is not bad, our perspectives are, mine is, in my lenses everything arrives in black and white and grey and brown and knowing it is the lens and not the world helps me exactly none. Feeling good feels impossible. Not sad, I would take sad, sad is a feeling and feelings move. This is failing to feel pleasure, the food is fine and the music is fine and the day off is fine and not one of them lands anywhere and this Poe themed autumn winter is inside me in August in a country where it is still thirty five degrees outside. People keep trying to change me and keep failing and then they get to be disappointed in me for being cold, which they say as though it were a decision I make every morning. So no, I did not manage the nice post again. But I have four hours back and I am going to be careful about what I do with them, because the running total so far is that I gave the first of them to a machine that watches me and the rest of them to this. And a couple of you actually wrote in with recommendations after I asked for a film last time. I have not managed to watch them yet but I am going to and I will say whether I liked them and if I cannot manage a whole review I will at least come back and write down whether I watched them or not. It feels kind of good that somebody is reading what I write.

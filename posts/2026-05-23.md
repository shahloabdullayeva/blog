Sometimes people ask me why i read. And what's annoying is that they ask that as if it's some kind of a hobby or something. It's not and they don't get it. They assume we enjoy it but we don't. Not always. Because they think we're sitting here having a great time when the main character is starving in the streets of Oslo? Or losing his mind over a crime in an apartment in St Petersburg? You think we enjoy that? Is it fun? It's painful actually and it affects our mood our mental health.
But the real question is why do we keep coming back to it. I think it's because reading is the only way to live inside someone else's mind. And feel what they feel. Understand why they made terrible decisions but still support them. And isn't that what we need right now. We live in a world where people don't talk to each other deeply anymore. We text. Send voice messages. And when we meet we say we're fine even if sometimes we're not.Yeah so i guess i read because someone once wrote down exactly what i feel that i didn't have words for.

I watched The Pear Tree tonight. It's a Dariush Mehrjui film from 1998. A writer named Mahmoud goes back to his family's garden in Damavand because he can't write anymore. There's an old pear tree in the yard that stopped producing fruit and he compares himself to it. And the whole film is just him sitting in that garden remembering. Remembering the person he loved when he was young. Remembering who he used to be and who has he become.

Some parts I loved from The pear tree:

A tempting silence has spread over the garden. The trees are awake. I can hear them breathe quietly.

و بعد خانه‌ای می‌بینم پر از گرمای فراموش‌شده، و پر از واقعیت فراموش کردن تو. پر از لحظه‌های رنگارنگ، پر از زمزمه‌های شیرین مهتاب. اما من کجا جا می‌شوم؟ کی در زندگی تو خواهم بود؟


And then I see a house full of the forgotten warmth, and full of the reality of you forgetting me. Full of so many colorful moments, full of sweet moonlight whispers. But where do I fit? When will I be in your life?

من پاییز را دوست ندارم. چرا همه فکر می‌کنند این فصل شعر است؟ من گرمای درخت گردو را با برگ‌های زرد و ریخته عوض نمی‌کنم. من به فصل انگور تعلق دارم و دارم می‌سوزم.

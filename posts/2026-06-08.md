# June 8, 2026

It's extremely hot. I know summer has to it's summerness but i really don't like it. I would rather die of cold rather than trying to sleep in this weather. It's not the "peak" of the summer even. And my brain has to come up with unbidden fantasies and weird, meaningless thoughts. If i could only make a deal with God i would ask to add a feature to manually turn off my mind...

Let me tell y'all about my russian teacher at school, I.A. He's the person who gave his life for teaching, to us, to the language. From 4th grade till 9 i used to go to russian olympiads and most i ever managed was 3rd place in Kosonsoy which was bad of course, but he was so happy about it because it was the most anyone from our school had ever brought back, and he carried it around like it was his own. He loved Yesenin and Pushkin and Lermontov, Dostoyevksy, Turgenev, Chekhov and Tolstoy, and Chameleon was his favourite, and he would ask us to read just a few lines, only a small part, and then he would tell the whole story himself, because the part on the page was always short but the part living inside him was endless and he would pour it out until the whole class went quiet and we did not always understand the words but we understood that he loved them, and somehow that was the real lesson. I hink he is the root of me, because everything i read now, Akhmatova and Tsvetaeva and Dostoevsky, all of it started in his classroom in those small parts he told whole, and he planted it there without either of us ever knowing that it would outlast the olympiads and the school and even the distance. And that is the thing i keep going back to tonight, the distance, because his house was only a few steps from the school and yet now i go home and i still cannot make myself walk those few small steps and i don't go, and maybe that is just what i do with the people i love the most, i keep meaning to reach them and i never quite arrive in time, and maybe writing him down here is the closest i have come to knocking on his door in years.

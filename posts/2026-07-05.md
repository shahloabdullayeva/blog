# July 5, 2026

Found out recently that Red Hat supports Israel. So I fallen in love with Fedora and I have been trying to learn it for almost a month now and things started to make sense. So Fedora is sponsored by RedHat and Red Hat is owned by IBM and it's not really a clean company as I consider. My first instinct was Fedora is open source, I don't pay for it, it's free and community version. So how could my using it profit them at all? It's a clean argument I would say but it's tied to Red Hat Enterprise Linux which is the one corporations pay money and Fedora is definitely a training ground. We are taught that value only means money, that if we are not swiping a card then we are not part of it, we are just watching from the side. But attention and data is value. Getting used to a brand is also value. Being one more body in the crowd that makes a thing look normal and permanent and inevitable is value. You can boycott with your wallet closed and still be feeding the machine with everything else you have. That's a genius approach actually but anyways.

And think about how normal all of it is. I live in Tashkent, there is a KFC that people gom there is Coca-Cola on every market, in every corner shop, at every wedding I have ever been to. Nobody reads the ownership chain before they drink. And I am not saying this from some clean height like I am better than any of them because I am not  and I do drink that exact Coke and stand in KFC line. The brands are so deep in ordinary life that stepping out of them does not feel normal, isn't it insane? It feels like telling people I will no longer breathe a particular kind of air. That is the true win of a corporation, the one they do not even have to fight for anymore. You can't even picture a normal life wihtout it.

Also there's a part where complicates things, sometimes boycotting works. In March of 2024 the group that runs Starbucks across the Middle East, Alshaya, laid off more than two thousand people because the boycott had hollowed out their sales, branches closed. By 2025 Starbucks had posted three straight quarters of falling revenue and said it would shut dozens more stores. McDonalds had its first real sales miss in about four years and stock dropped by something close to seven billion dollars. In some Arab countries its sales fell somewhere between fifty and ninety percent in a single month. It got bad enough that McDonalds bought its entire Israeli franchise back it was 200+ restaurants if I'm not mistaken.

So why can't I just go and make tea instead of Coke? I got my own reasons: 2K+ people were fired in Amman and Cairo and Kuwait who decided nothing about the war who just needed a job and feed their families. So boycott worked against normal people and those people on top will retire just fine. The money you didn't spend on Starbucks goes to some other coffee shop, which is owned by another fund and if you had time to trace it down you would also boycott. IBM, Intel, Dell, HP, Nestlé, Chevron, the bank that holds your loan, the phone in your palm, the very laptop I am typing this into right now which is HP, which has it's chips produced in companies that support Israeli government. It's like water, this whole damn system that's built and we're already wet. And this is why South Africa does not save me even though it technically could. Apartheid was one regime, one government you could push on until it cracked. What I am talking about is not a regime. It is the whole arrangement underneath all the regimes, the machine that simply reassembles itself out of whatever pieces are lying around. You can boycott a government. I do not think you can boycott an economy and I don't even have enough vocabulary base to explain all this.

So what do I do. I do not know and I will probably keep using Fedora, because the alternative is not purity, it is only a different flavor of the same compromise wearing a different name. Just to keep my own soul legible to myself. Because that is the thing I keep circling back to and I will just say it plainly and stop dressing it up. Whatever we do, the world does not change from here. It is too far gone and corrupted the whole way down rotten at a root that none of us can reach with anything we have. We can make ourselves feel a little cleaner by not using Apple products lets say.

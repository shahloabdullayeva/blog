# October 6, 2026

I could not write again. I want to be clear that this time there is no excuse, because last time the excuse was work and this time I had a whole week with nobody's schedule in it but mine and I still did not write. I could not even read. I wanted to, I really did. I wanted to read The Martian because it sounded like exactly the right kind of book for a week like this, a man alone on a planet solving one problem after another with nothing but stubbornness and bad jokes and I thought if anything can pull me in it is that. I downloaded the PDF. That is as far as it went. It is still sitting in my downloads with the other things I meant to start, and every time I thought about opening it something in me just did not. I do not know why. It is not the book's fault, it never even got the chance. I think it is not anybody's fault, which is the most annoying kind of thing to be stuck with.

Today is the last day of my days off and I am glad to report that they were not wasted. I had time to myself, which was the whole point and I made real progress on Kaido, more than I expected to. It has a dark mode now, which sounds small but I have been staring at that white screen at midnight for a month. Drivers can do their pre-trip inspection from a link on their phone, with a photo for every item, so nobody can tick boxes from the bed anymore and something actually looks at the photos. It has a little pixel cat in the corner now that walks out and brings you notes, which nobody asked for except me. And today I connected a second tracking system to it, so the trucks that one system could not see now show up from the other and the driver list that was a mess of half names and duplicates finally looks like real people. It is not glamorous. Nobody will ever write a book about it. But I built it and it works and that is a feeling I do not get from much else lately.

Now the actual thing I sat down to say. I do not think I have a personal opinion. Every time I think I have one and I look at it closely, it is borrowed. It is from a book or a movie or a song and I just wore it long enough that it started to feel like mine. Even when I try to describe myself I end up quoting somebody. Every single thing I touch becomes sick with sadness. All my mornings are Mondays, stuck in an endless February. With insomnia nothing is real, everything is far away. Those are not my sentences. They are the sentences I reached for because they already said it better than I could and maybe that is all I am, a collection of other people's lines in the right order. And the worst part is that the line that describes this best is borrowed too, from the same movie the insomnia one came from, everything is a copy of a copy of a copy.

But I will be back. I always come back, that is the one thing about me I am sure nobody lent me.
